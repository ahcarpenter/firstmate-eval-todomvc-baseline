## Ask
Let people rename and delete lists. Beside each list's link, add a "Rename <list name>" button and a "Delete <list name>" button (for a list called Work, "Rename Work" and "Delete Work"). Rename shows a field holding the list's name, focused; typing a new name and pressing Enter renames the list, and Escape cancels. Delete asks for confirmation with the browser's confirm dialog, then removes the list and its todos; if it was the open list, the app switches to the first remaining list. The last remaining list cannot be deleted. Renames and deletions are kept across reloads.
